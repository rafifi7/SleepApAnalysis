"""
KEY VARIABLES DICTIONARY FOR SLEEP APNEA STUDY
===============================================

This explains what each variable means and how it's coded in NHANES.
Always check the official NHANES documentation PDFs for complete details.


IDENTIFIER
----------
SEQN: Respondent sequence number (unique ID for each person)


DEMOGRAPHICS (DEMO_J.xpt)
--------------------------
RIDAGEYR: Age in years at screening
  - Range: 0-80 (80+ are coded as 80)

RIAGENDR: Gender
  - 1 = Male
  - 2 = Female

RIDRETH3: Race/Hispanic origin with NH Asian
  - 1 = Mexican American
  - 2 = Other Hispanic
  - 3 = Non-Hispanic White
  - 4 = Non-Hispanic Black
  - 6 = Non-Hispanic Asian
  - 7 = Other Race (including multi-racial)


INCOME & SOCIOECONOMIC STATUS
------------------------------
INDFMPIR: Ratio of family income to poverty (from DEMO_J)
  - Range: 0 to 5
  - Values close to 0 = very poor
  - Values at or above 5 = income is 5+ times poverty threshold
  - This is your main INCOME variable!

INDHHIN2: Total household income (from INQ_J)
  - 1 = $0 to $4,999
  - 2 = $5,000 to $9,999
  - 3 = $10,000 to $14,999
  - 4 = $15,000 to $19,999
  - 5 = $20,000 to $24,999
  - 6 = $25,000 to $34,999
  - 7 = $35,000 to $44,999
  - 8 = $45,000 to $54,999
  - 9 = $55,000 to $64,999
  - 10 = $65,000 to $74,999
  - 12 = $20,000 and Over (combined category)
  - 13 = Under $20,000 (combined category)
  - 14 = $75,000 to $99,999
  - 15 = $100,000 and Over
  - 77 = Refused
  - 99 = Don't know


HOUSING CONDITIONS (HOQ_J.xpt)
-------------------------------
HOD050: Number of rooms in home
  - Range: 1-13
  - 13 = 13 or more rooms

HOQ065: Home owned, bought, rented, or occupied
  - 1 = Owned or being bought
  - 2 = Rented
  - 3 = Other arrangement
  - 7 = Refused
  - 9 = Don't know


HEALTH INSURANCE / ACCESS (HIQ_J.xpt)
--------------------------------------
HIQ011: Are you covered by health insurance?
  - 1 = Yes
  - 2 = No
  - 7 = Refused
  - 9 = Don't know

HIQ031A: Are you covered by private insurance?
  - 14 = Covered by private insurance
  - 77 = Refused
  - 99 = Don't know

HIQ031B: Are you covered by Medicare?
  - 15 = Covered by Medicare
  - 77 = Refused
  - 99 = Don't know

HIQ031D: Are you covered by Medicaid/CHIP?
  - 17 = Covered by Medicaid/CHIP
  - 77 = Refused
  - 99 = Don't know


SLEEP DISORDERS (SLQ_J.xpt) - YOUR PRIMARY OUTCOMES
----------------------------------------------------
SLQ050: Ever told a doctor or other health professional that you have trouble sleeping?
  - 1 = Yes  ← KEY VARIABLE FOR SLEEP DISORDER DIAGNOSIS
  - 2 = No
  - 7 = Refused
  - 9 = Don't know

SLQ030: How often do you snore while sleeping?
  - 0 = Never
  - 1 = Rarely - 1-2 nights a week
  - 2 = Occasionally - 3-4 nights a week
  - 3 = Frequently - 5 or more nights a week
  - 7 = Refused
  - 9 = Don't know

SLQ040: How often do you snort, gasp, or stop breathing?
  - 0 = Never
  - 1 = Rarely - 1-2 nights a week
  - 2 = Occasionally - 3-4 nights a week
  - 3 = Frequently - 5 or more nights a week
  - 7 = Refused
  - 9 = Don't know
  - Note: This is a KEY indicator of sleep apnea!

SLQ120: How often do you feel overly sleepy during the day?
  - 0 = Never
  - 1 = Rarely - 1 time a month
  - 2 = Sometimes - 2-4 times a month
  - 3 = Often - 5-15 times a month
  - 4 = Almost always - 16-30 times a month
  - 7 = Refused
  - 9 = Don't know

SLD012: Sleep hours - weekdays or workdays
  - Range: 2-14 hours
  - Continuous variable

SLD013: Sleep hours - weekends
  - Range: 2-14 hours
  - Continuous variable


BODY MEASURES (BMX_J.xpt)
--------------------------
BMXBMI: Body Mass Index (kg/m²)
  - Continuous variable
  - Important confounder - obesity is major risk factor for sleep apnea
  - <18.5 = Underweight
  - 18.5-24.9 = Normal
  - 25-29.9 = Overweight
  - 30+ = Obese


CARDIOVASCULAR (CDQ_J.xpt)
---------------------------
CDQ001: Has a doctor ever told you that you had chest pain?
  - 1 = Yes
  - 2 = No
  - 7 = Refused
  - 9 = Don't know
  - Important comorbidity related to sleep apnea


SURVEY WEIGHTS (ESSENTIAL FOR ANALYSIS!)
-----------------------------------------
WTMEC2YR: Full sample 2-year MEC exam weight
  - Use this when analyzing exam/lab variables (like BMI)

WTINT2YR: Full sample 2-year interview weight
  - Use this when analyzing questionnaire-only variables

SDMVPSU: Masked variance pseudo-PSU
  - Needed for variance estimation

SDMVSTRA: Masked variance pseudo-stratum
  - Needed for variance estimation

WHY WEIGHTS MATTER:
NHANES oversamples certain groups (elderly, minorities, low-income).
Without weights, your results won't represent the US population!


MISSING DATA CODES
------------------
Common missing/invalid codes across variables:
- 7, 77, 777 = Refused
- 9, 99, 999 = Don't know
- . (blank) = Missing

YOU MUST RECODE THESE AS MISSING (NaN) BEFORE ANALYSIS!


RESEARCH TIPS
-------------
1. Your main outcome: SLQ050 (ever told doctor about sleep trouble)
2. Secondary indicators: SLQ030, SLQ040 (snoring, stop breathing)
3. Main predictors: INDFMPIR (income), HIQ011 (insurance), HOQ065 (housing)
4. Important confounder: BMXBMI (obesity causes sleep apnea!)
5. Always use survey weights in your analysis


EXAMPLE RESEARCH QUESTIONS YOU CAN ANSWER
------------------------------------------
- Do people with lower income have higher rates of sleep disorders?
- Does having health insurance affect sleep apnea diagnosis rates?
- How do housing conditions relate to sleep quality?
- What's the relationship between BMI and sleep apnea symptoms?
- Do uninsured people report more untreated sleep problems?
"""
